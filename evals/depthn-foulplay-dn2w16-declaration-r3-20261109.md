# screen-r3: DN2W16 against our RAW arm on the fixed search tree, servers on a separate node, minimum-support fairness (declaration notes)

**Pre-registered panel declaration:** `depthn-foulplay-dn2w16-declaration-r3-20261109.json`, sha256 `3a05d247ab84e8e240fbd0733c10f3189626e70386a9076499a9587c7c0c3dbd`. The panel validates it with its own rules (`panel.validate`, `check_preflight`). The panel's schema is closed, so the conditions below that it cannot carry are recorded here.

## What screen-r3 asks
The same question as screen-r2: was screen-r1's result caused by the broken search inputs, or does search lose to Foul Play anyway? Same operator (DN2W16, ARGMAX_MEAN_COMPLETED_Q, 3 s cap), same corrected tree and reach guard, same 500 cells (screen-r1's seeds), RAW re-run.

## Why screen-r3 exists
screen-r2 (declaration `45cbb471…`, run `/out/screen-r2`) ended **VOID_FAIRNESS** at 150 pairs. Its arm-fairness flag 2 (drift) failed even under the outcome-blind exact-alive-bands amendment (window 1.098, whole run 1.055 against 5%); the lead acknowledged it as a fault (`void.json`). **No screen-r2 outcome has been read.** Its result files stay sealed (`/out/screen-r2-SEALED.json`: 328 result names, names sha256 `ab464c69…`) until screen-r3 has a verdict; after that they may be reported only labelled VOID, as context. The imbalance sat in sparse lopsided-endgame cells (for example ours 1 vs theirs 2: 2.08 on 8/38 moves; ours 2 vs theirs 1: 0.27 on 78/26), while matched strata read 0.96–1.02.

## What changes from screen-r2 (and only this)
1. **Arm fairness rule: `RULE_R3`** (`pokezero.depthn_live_fairness.RULE_R3`, declared as `arm_fairness`):
   - strata: Foul Play budget stratum × turn band × our exact alive band × their exact alive band, bands {1}, {2}, {3-4}, {5-6} (0 joins 1), from the owner's owning-wire capture;
   - the official sparse merge of adjacent turn bands within each (budget, ours, theirs) group, at `min_cell_moves` 120;
   - **minimum support:** a merged cell enters the pooled-count-weighted ratio only with ≥ 120 rated Foul Play moves in each arm; the reading reports the unsupported cells and each arm's supported share;
   - unchanged: tolerance 5%, coverage gap 5%, floors (50 pairs, 1,000 moves per arm), checkpoints every 50 pairs, re-arm margin 2.5%, the flag logic.
   - The same rule runs the panel's in-run checkpoints (cumulative and drift), the readout's fairness reading and the final gate (`scripts/depthn_fairness_final.py final --rule r3`).
2. **N = 120 and how it was chosen.** The derivation uses screen-r1's fairness data only: about 30 independent games per arm per cell (the usual floor for a game-clustered mean) at screen-r1's median of 3.7 (RAW) and 3.9 (search) Foul Play moves per game in its sparse exact-band groups, so 30 × 3.8 ≈ 114, rounded to 120. N was fixed before any screen-r2 reading under `RULE_R3` was computed. **Disclosure:** when N was chosen, screen-r2's per-cell exact-alive-band diagnosis of its flag-2 window (cell sizes and ratios, fairness data only) had already been seen, so screen-r2 informed the choice; see the sensitivity below.
3. **Placement:** the search servers run in their own pod (4 GPUs on one machine), on a machine different from the worker pod's and from the Foul Play collection's, enforced by a hard scheduling constraint, as screen-r1 had them. In screen-r2 the launcher's default co-location preference had put the servers on the workers' machine. Workers stay on the calibrated core pairs of the same worker pod as screen-r2.
4. **Server tree** `e775c02084045916103152847b4c9605ab8506dc` = screen-r2's server tree `c7808ad9` plus the screen-r2 amendment records and the `RULE_R3` code (fairness module, panel, final-gate script, tests). The search, guard and actor code is screen-r2's. Dispatch runs the declaration commit `c2c94a4142c08f53169a89da3ce31c2843ef5be0`, which differs from the server tree by the declaration JSON alone (`server_tree_check`).
5. **Pre-flight** re-run on the new tree and placement (`/out/preflight-s3/preflight.json`, sha256 `47a4230c514633dcea73b8ce92a33576240b9ce684aefbdf783be23437b08a34`): 24 games, outcome-blind, on this placement. Search wall p50 1.82 s, p95 2.34 s (screen-r2: 2.52 s); timeouts 0 (screen-r2: 0.44%); searched share 82.0% of non-timeout decisions (screen-r2: 75.0%); constructor refusals 7.9%. The search arm's warm greedy p95 fell from 0.56 s to 0.27 s with the servers off the worker node. The declared warm p95 is the RAW arm's (as `check_preflight` requires): encode 0.041 s, greedy 0.256 s; `panel.init` checks it matches the pre-flight and it feeds no decision rule.
6. **Unchanged from screen-r2:** calibration (`/out/calibration-s2`, MEASURED 1.130 / 0.902) and move walls (the lead: the worker pod's calibration and move walls stay valid), the journal-full base config (`49a8a21f…`), engine tables, arms, interleave, cells, deadline and reserve, the exact reach guard and its conditions (screen-r2's note, conditions 1–4), breakage and latency rules, confidence 0.9.

Artifacts in the run's output storage: `/out/fairness-r3/min-support-r1r2-n120.json` (sha256 `d2185e7f…`), produced by `min_support_analysis.py` (sha256 `3cf22a38…`).
- **screen-r1 (500 pairs; it was fair):** under `RULE_R3`, no cumulative checkpoint flags (largest |difference| 2.0%); final gate +2.00% (within), supported share 99.0% RAW / 99.3% search. The committed gate (`final --rule r3`) reproduces +2.0045% to 1e-16. Under the declared screen-r1 rule its checkpoints had flagged at 200 pairs (0.950) and in a drift window (0.862).
- **screen-r2 at 150 pairs (for the record only):** checkpoints at 50/100/150 pairs read +1.1%, +1.1%, +3.1% (no flag); the whole run +3.1% (within); supported share 88% / 91%. Computed after N was fixed. Sensitivity: at N = 30 it would flag at 150 pairs (5.6%), at N = 75 too (5.1%); N ≥ 100 passes.

## Decision rules
The official readout is the panel's, computed from a clean checkout of the dispatch commit. The verdict is `scripts/depthn_screen_amended_verdict.py --run-binding` with screen-r1's four committed amendments, adopted by `evals/depthn-foulplay-dn2w16-amendment-screen-r3-adoption-20261109.json` (sha256 `facd0798ea97d2732811aad1baaf8ccd325809bc152503a28acef2d7bc205f05`, pinned in `RUN_BINDINGS` and `RUN_FINAL_RULES`), which binds them to this declaration, `/out/screen-r3` and the dispatch tree, and declares `final_fairness_rule: RULE_R3`: the final-fairness amendment's VOID rule applies to the `RULE_R3` gate (|difference| > 5%: VOID_FAIRNESS). No run amendment is adopted or allowed. No extension to 2,000 cells is declared.
- **Mid-run fairness flags:** any flag that fails `RULE_R3` stops the run and goes to the lead. No amendment mid-run.
- **Missing captures:** a Foul Play move without its owner capture would be read with both sides at 6 alive. The final gate counts the COMPLETE cells whose owner capture file is missing, and the verdict refuses to run (no verdict, not VOID) when that count is above 0. Rounds beyond the end of a capture are report-only (`band_from_round`, per arm). The in-run checkpoints report `band_from_round`; the captures sit at their recorded paths on the worker pod.
