# Final bounded Phase D constructor tail

This is the final source batch under the original coverage plan, following
batch 2 at source `2f15d837`. Its only additions are positively resolved faint
replacement requests, positively fresh Substitute, and public Wish state.
The target is 90% overall on the same 200 frozen roots at 16 and 32 worlds.

then the constructor program stops regardless of the result. Required private

## Public replacement phase

A literal owning `forceSwitch: [true]` packet must have six members, one active
fainted member, no active move request and a matching public `force-switch`
payload. Living pivots and Baton Pass remain refused. The current displayed
turn must exist in the complete owning prefix. Every surviving actor must
already have public action/BeforeMove evidence, or the complete upkeep must
have occurred; a dead actor's unused choice is canceled. An unidentified live
queue, passive displacement, missing phase/arrival facts or partial residual
processing remains a named refusal. Public `upkeep` determines whether the
residual remains pending. No committed move is guessed from a sampled set.

The original generic `map_spec` requires a living ordinary cursor. The Phase D
adapter therefore selects an existing living party member solely while mapping
storage, preserving every party member's literal HP and status. No Pokémon or
HP is fabricated. Active effects that cannot safely be relocated refuse. Actual
fainted cursors, last-action coordinates, forced-switch flags, pending residual,
public turn and active ages are restored before own comparisons, legal use,
public carry or any world return. Public fresh Substitute and sleep restoration
are likewise completed before exposure. Last-move information cannot leak onto
the temporary storage cursor. The original generic mapper is unchanged.

When the public residual is pending, `transition.sample_chance_inputs(state)`
provides the actual frozen finite law under the existing per-world kernel key
and replacement-count domain. Its actual draw, phazing, secondary and Sleep Talk
arrays populate the suspended capsule. These are **sampled hypothetical future
randomness**, never recovered original-turn random draws or a native-state
claim. The constructor does not call a battle transition or rewrite the engine.
The unchanged legacy `phase_d_chance.SOURCE_PINS` names a different transition
hash at this baseline; this pre-existing exact-consumer certificate failure
remains disclosed and is not bypassed by the sampled constructor.

Existing B2 current damage sums are copied into the existing damage history:
shown foe damage remains shown; exact own-victim fractions remain owner-known.
High/low compensated parts are retained. Unavailable peer-private fractions stay
redacted, and reply-private-history availability remains false. Actual public
faint/presence, Wish history and turn must agree between B2 and payload before
the corresponding guard is discharged.

## Positively fresh Substitute

A current Substitute needs all of: matching active species, the public
`-start` creation in its current stint, payload `substituteHealthState: full`,
zero integer depletion/minimum depletion, matching origin, and corroborated B2
presence. A hit/activation, transfer, different origin, missing provenance or
nonzero depletion refuses. Damaged and passed Substitutes are outside this batch,
including an exact public damage amount. No hidden HP or hit posterior is added.

A value copy defers the positively proven Substitute while the strict typed
bridge maps storage; the physical active's HP is then exactly its world-specific
`max_hp // 4`. Own maximum HP is request-proven; an opponent's maximum belongs
to that hypothetical sampled world. Source payload, original posterior and B2
history remain unchanged. Other active volatiles retain their existing guards.

## Wish: correction from the pinned primary source

The advisor/coverage-plan statement that Gen 3 Wish heals from the caster's
maximum HP is contradicted by the pinned source. At Showdown
`f76228a1354b5d0f307ca2d16101294ad3a2308b`:

- `data/mods/gen3/scripts.ts` inherits `gen4`; Gen 3 has no Wish override.
- `data/mods/gen4/moves.ts:1495` defines duration 2, residual order 7, and heals
  `target.baseMaxhp / 2` in `onEnd`.
- `sim/battle.ts:2277` truncates healing, with the positive sub-unit minimum.

The frozen numerical `sustain.wish_residual` agrees: it heals the **resolving
recipient's** maximum HP divided by two, truncated and at least one. The frozen
transition passes that recipient's HP and maximum; its stored `wish_amount`
is not an input to this residual. The contrary comment in the shared native
bridge is not used as a mechanics authority and is left unchanged in this batch.

The constructor retains original public Wish set-turns only while their age is
0 or 1, checks agreement with B2, and restores counter `2 - age` on both seats.
For the existing compatibility field it records half the current recipient's
maximum, without inventing a caster maximum. Later switching continues to use
the frozen recipient rule. Missing or contradictory public timing stays named.
No engine or item/ability mechanics are changed.
