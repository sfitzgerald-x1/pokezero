October 18, 2026. Completed exploration, not holdout confirmation.

## Frozen measurement and completion

The incumbent public-belief selector used four worlds at 1,024 native
iterations each, 4,096 total. These are production work counters, not
independently qualified atomic-backup counts or a one-second budget. Raw
argmax drove both source seats. Every eligible nonopening p1 boundary was
scanned in frozen chronological order.

Only full-budget search deviations within the inclusive margin band received
eight paired common-random-number continuations, followed by raw-policy
play. Margin is raw's largest minus second-largest legal probability,
without renormalization; one legal action has undefined margin. The stop was
the first 50 provisional band deviations or eight calendar hours, with no
scanned-root count cap. Nomination additionally required 32 validated sources,
20 validated deviations, complete prior gates and a strictly positive
primary one-sided lower bound.

The run reached its deviation target: 2,654 scanned roots from 58 validated
sources, including 412 in-band roots and 50 in-band deviations from 24 sources.
Worker, supervisor and actual outer execution all exited zero, with no cleanup
error. The original roster will not be resumed or extended.

Identification bounds describe this finite sample's missing-outcome
uncertainty; they are not confidence intervals. The primary lower bound was
negative, so the registered positive-bound rule did not nominate a candidate.
The secondary weighting cannot replace that primary. This establishes neither
equivalence nor a negative true effect, and does not diagnose the value head.

Of 800 action cells, 799 were validated COMPLETE and one was CAPPED with a null
score under the 250-boundary continuation cap. Forty-nine deviation contrasts
were fully identified; all 50 remained in the conditional denominator. The
capped cell was neither retried, dropped nor scored as a half-win. There were
no unknown-membership roots or source-validation failures.

The 10,000 source-seed resamples used seed 1979079761264254766 and retained
all 58 clusters, including the 34 with no band deviations. An empty denominator
would retain [-1, 1], never be discarded; none occurred. Other margin bands
have action counts but no requested continuation labels, so their effects
remain unestimated. The adaptive bootstrap remains descriptive and uncertified.
