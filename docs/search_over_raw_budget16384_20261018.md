## Measurement and completion

The incumbent model leaf used four public-belief worlds at 4,096 native
iterations each. Raw argmax drove both source seats. Every eligible
nonopening p1 boundary was scanned in frozen chronological order; each
search deviation received eight paired common-random-number continuations
per unique action, followed by raw-policy play. The stop was 100 provisional
deviations or eight calendar hours, not a scanned-root quota.

The fixed sequence stopped after 726 roots: 626 raw-action agreements and
100 deviations. All 15 source gates passed and source-validation failures
were empty. Worker, supervisor and actual outer execution exited zero;
there was no cleanup error. The roster will not resume or be extended to
reach 32 sources.

Of 1,600 continuation cells, 1,594 were validated COMPLETE and six were
CAPPED with null scores under the 250-boundary continuation limit. Those
six affect five deviation roots: 95 deviation contrasts are fully identified,
but all 100 remain in their denominator. Overall, 721 of 726 contrasts are
identified; all 726 remain in the primary denominator. No capped cell was
retried, dropped or scored as a half-win. Deviation membership is known for
every root.
