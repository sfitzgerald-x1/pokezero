# Search versus raw at 1024 fixed iterations

October 17, 2026. Completed exploration, not holdout confirmation.

Model-leaf search changed raw's action on eight of 200 roots from 32 fresh
source seeds: one positive continuation contrast, three negative and four
zero. Search did not demonstrate an advantage.

## Measurement

| Model search versus raw | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Root-weighted mean in percentage points | -0.1875 |
| Descriptive seed-clustered 95% interval in points | -0.5597 to +0.0638 |
| Equal-seed estimate in points | -0.1953 |
| Mean across eight deviations in points | -4.6875 |

The interval uses 10,000 source-seed resamples, retaining their roots,
with bootstrap seed 20261016. Eight deviations fall below the required
20-deviation screening minimum. This result establishes neither equivalence
nor reliable harm. The [320 panel](search_over_raw_live320_20261016.md)
used different fresh sources; these panels do not isolate a budget effect.

The live public-belief selector used four worlds with 256 native iterations
each, 1,024 total: production work counters, not independently qualified
atomic-backup counts or a one-second budget. Raw drove both source seats.
Each unique selected action received eight paired common-random-number
continuations, followed by raw-policy play.

There were 1,660 validated completed cells and four capped cells, with no
refused, unstarted or unvalidated-COMPLETE cells. Capped scores remain null.
All four caps occur on three identical-action roots, where shared-action
cancellation identifies the contrasts without imputing outcomes. All 200
contrasts are identified; collapsed equal-seed identification bounds concern
this finite roster, not the population.
